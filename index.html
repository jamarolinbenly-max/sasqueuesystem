⚙️ Setup Instructions

  1. Step 1: Open "Admin Dashboard" on your desk/tablet - keep it open
  2. Step 2: Share the "Join Queue" link or display QR code at your entrance
  3. Step 3: When customers click the link or scan QR, they enter their name
  4. Step 4: Their name appears instantly in your Admin Dashboard
  5. Step 5: Click "Call Next" to announce and display the next customer
